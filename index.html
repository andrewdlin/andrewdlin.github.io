About

Welcome to my academic website. I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at University Name. My research focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and natural language processing.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University in 2015 and joined University Name in 2016. Prior to that, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics at MIT.

Research Interests

My primary research areas include:

Machine learning algorithms and their applications to real-world problems, with a particular focus on deep learning architectures. I am also interested in natural language understanding and computational linguistics, exploring how machines can better comprehend human language. Additionally, my work spans computer vision and image recognition, developing systems that can interpret visual information with increasing accuracy.
